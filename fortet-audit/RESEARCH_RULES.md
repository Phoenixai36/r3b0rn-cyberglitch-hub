# RESEARCH RULES — FORTET / NONA

1. Never alter primary source files.
2. Keep PRINTED, ERRATA-CORRECTED and MATHEMATICALLY-COMPUTED data separate.
3. Every derived artifact must identify its inputs and transformation.
4. Never tune a transformation after observing a target.
5. Freeze discovery rules before validation.
6. Separate discovery and validation sets.
7. Record all parameters and random seeds.
8. Run appropriate null-model controls.
9. Treat visual similarity as hypothesis generation, not proof.
10. Historical claims require historical evidence.
11. Mathematical claims require reproducible computation.
12. Preserve negative, failed and contradictory results.
13. Do not assume 8181, 618, 1618, 50, Capella or Auriga are intended targets.
14. Treat previous AI-generated reports as hypothesis material until independently verified.
15. Prefer the simplest rule consistent with the evidence.
16. If a hypothesis needs increasingly specific exceptions, flag probable overfitting.
17. Log provenance and hashes whenever technically possible.
18. Make every analysis rerunnable from a clean checkout.
19. Never silently reconcile conflicting source values; record the conflict.
20. The project may legitimately conclude that there is insufficient evidence for intentional encoding.

OUTPUT STANDARD
source -> rule -> calculation -> control -> prediction -> replication

Anything that does not satisfy that chain remains an explicit hypothesis.
